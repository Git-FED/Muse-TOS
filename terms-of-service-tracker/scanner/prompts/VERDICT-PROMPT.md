# Verdict Prompt

> Turn this scan comparison into a finished verdict using `templates/verdict-template.md`. Fill every field: action, platform, verdict, rules cited (exact text + source + snapshot date), what the verdict means for the user in normal words, and what to do next (go ahead, change the action, ask the mods in writing, or do not do it).
